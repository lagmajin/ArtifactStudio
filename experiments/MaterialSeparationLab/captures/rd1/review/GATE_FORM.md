RD1 notes for the reviewer: the run compares five candidates (A guided two-layer decomposition x2, B frequency separation, C emphasis model x2) that split an image into base and detail layers and recombine them with independent gains. The descriptor contract (straight vs premultiplied input) already returns an exact match for all candidates, and the neutral setting reproduces the input to float rounding, so 5.1 and 5.3 are contract checks rather than quality judgements. What still needs a human tolerance is how much detail gain may change a flat area before it reads as noise, how much halo the recomposition may introduce around the alpha boundary, and whether the emphasis model is preferable to the plain separation models. Judge the blind sheet for texture presence, halo and doubled motif, not the numeric differences alone.
